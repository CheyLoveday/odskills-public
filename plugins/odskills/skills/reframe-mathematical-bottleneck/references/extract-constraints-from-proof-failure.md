# Extract constraints from proof failure

Translate the failed test into its exact algebraic predicate. Specify the
matrix, orientation, coefficient ring, determinant or minors, and rank
threshold. Give an exhaustive set of branches. Derive the actual consequence
from each branch and mark any unresolved branch before recombining.

Rational proportionality of integral rows does not identify the allowed
integer multiples. Recover a primitive vector, saturation or the index,
and prove the congruence or integral divisibility asserted by the application.
For full rank, nonvanishing gives a positive integer lower bound after exact
clearing. A bound that grows with a parameter needs a proved divisibility
factor or another quantitative argument. Retain any branch for which the
required lower bound is open.
