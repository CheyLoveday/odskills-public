# Compress counting through shared generators

Define the target objects, identity and weights. A catalogue may count
factors, curves, intervals or labels while the theorem counts distinct
points, incidences or source occurrences. Write the relation between a
generator and its manifestations, and prove the coverage and multiplicity
facts needed in the direction of the bound. Use a weighted sum of fibre
bounds when the fibres are nonuniform. Track overlaps or uncovered objects
at the source.

For polynomial factors, state the chosen height, primitive normalisation,
degree range and any losses under multiplication or factorisation. A count
of bounded-height generators alone does not control the number of target
objects. Compare the transferred count with the old bound.
