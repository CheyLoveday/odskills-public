# Amplify invariants through lifts

Identify which quantity a single relation bounds and which stronger estimate
the enlargement must yield. Give the lift map and check that it preserves
the original source, arithmetic constraints and height convention. Compute
the rank of the lifted rows or columns. Distinguish genuinely independent
relations from repeats or integer multiples.

Track the scale of the nonzero minors through clearing, changes of basis,
and passage to primitive or saturated data. When using a lattice comparison,
prove the applicable index formula and lower bound for the primitive minors.
Rank alone supplies no height gain. If the consumer needs a power such as
$H^k$, show that power after common factors, degree and norm costs have been
accounted for. Keep the original representation if the net improvement
vanishes.
