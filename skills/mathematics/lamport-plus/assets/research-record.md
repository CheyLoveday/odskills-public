# Research record

- Claim ID, canonical name and revision
- Exact statement, declared types, quantifiers, hypotheses and conclusion
- Downstream use and the exact bridge it needs
- Inputs and dependencies, with their evidence and source revisions
- Backward and forward deductions, analogies or disconfirmable predictions
- Candidates, checked counterexamples, revisions and which claims they affect
- Planned checks and expected results, including adverse and held-out controls
- Executed checks, coverage, commands, input or revision IDs, and receipts
- Proof status, exact open leaves and blocked dependents
- Links to the proof, registry, actual formal evidence and reader assessment
